How The Ecosystem Works

A closed-loop, verifiable flow from setup to daily bus boarding

1

Vendor Configures Transport Network

Vendor creates Organizations, sets up Routes with stop fees, adds Buses with capacities, and creates Conductors with auto-generated IDs.

2

Student Applies & Pays Securely

Student selects organization, route, and stop, reviews fee, scans vendor UPI QR, and submits transaction reference with receipt screenshot proof.

3

Vendor Verifies & Issues Digital Pass

Vendor verifies payment screenshot, approves application, and the system automatically generates an encrypted Digital Bus Pass with dynamic QR code.

4

Conductor Validates Boarding & Streams GPS

Conductor starts live duty on mobile, broadcasting live GPS to student & vendor maps. Conductor scans student QR code on boarding for instant verification.
